Kozhikode

Kozhikode, also historically known as Calicut, is a major coastal city located on the southwestern Malabar Coast of Kerala, India. Famed as the ancient "City of Spices", it has been a legendary maritime trading hub for centuries, connecting India with Arab, Chinese, African, and European merchants.
